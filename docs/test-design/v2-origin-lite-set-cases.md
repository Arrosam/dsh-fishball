### Common fixtures and accepted limits

- All addresses and identities below are **synthetic fixture data**, not inspected services: main `http://127.0.0.1:54679`, child generation `owned-A` at `http://127.0.0.1:47131`, replacement `owned-B` at `http://127.0.0.1:47132`, unrelated trusted-process fixture at port `47133`; manager `session-fishball-case`, ordinary fixture `session-other-case`.
- Native launch token and signed cookies are generated by a future disposable native fixture, not hardcoded real credentials. A token is reusable per native process; do **not** assert one-use native admission. Synthetic secret sentinels are for sink inspection only.
- Main-origin localStorage has a nonempty shortcut preference and unrelated sentinel; child has its own nonempty preferences, including unrelated commands. Compare main before/after byte-for-byte and observe that main receives no child-caused storage update. Do not reset either store. Same host cookies may cross ports: this trusted-own-DSH-process scope is accepted, while signature/audience/expiry remain enforced.
- Native CSP/framing defaults are accepted, including no exact-main `frame-ancestors` enforcement. No response proxy is required or permitted as a workaround. Remote-main-only tunnels, different-site framing and mixed-content deployment limitations are documented, **not failure partitions demanding remote support or malicious-sibling-port cookie isolation**.
- Only bypass-UI/unreachable native races are accepted limitations. An ordinary user-reachable failing control must be handled honestly; it cannot be relabelled an edge case. The tests do not demand a nonexistent precreation RPC veto.
- Private Skills is the sole narrow agent-write exception. These cases verify only its authority boundary; six-operation, filesystem publication and catalogue behavior belong to SK. Recommendation tab internals belong to RP. No V3 feature, migration or bundled-plugin acceptance is introduced.
