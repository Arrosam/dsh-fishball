## Iterator/currentness interruption detail

The barrier is consumer backpressure at a real public seam, not an artificial upstream stream: the approved adapter describes a bounded non-streaming HTTP response followed by original DSH chunks. Install a scoped public llm/stream observer before the genuine installed execute; receive the actual frozen original GenerateOptions and call its next() once to obtain the real iterable. Delegate its real next/return operations and record only allowlisted chunk kinds, counts, digests and signal/lifecycle observations. Do not copy options, stamp a marker, synthesize chunks, change native authority callbacks or patch Agent/NativeAdapter/WorkerManager methods.

When the real block-start is observed, suspend before asking that same iterator for its next chunk. Begin original owner control(cancel) concurrently. Do not wait for control to finish while blocking the consumer: original cancellation awaits idle. Observe the actual AbortSignal event or the original durable control/currentness transition, release the consumer barrier, and attempt resume/close on the real iterator. The refused captured guard can refuse next/throw/return; record that actual refusal, preserve unknown drain/termination holds, and do not claim an unconditional cleanup acknowledgement. Then await the existing cancellation/idle/owned disposal path only within the bounded run and inspect original public status/ledger facts. The required post-loss oracle is no successful model content/usage/completion admitted under lost authority, with no captured authoritative result created from late chunks. Already-observed bytes and remotely completed response facts must be preserved rather than claimed undone. The entire path needs a bounded latch and honest cleanup timeout, not a test-only lifecycle overwrite or forced release.

A nominal 60000 ms transport deadline is not an instant-cancel or global cloud-stop guarantee. The design sets an outer live window of at most 480000 ms and an R06 window of at most 90000 ms, including barrier/cleanup observation; deadline expiry records HOLD/unknown and grants no release or replay. Root must seal these bounds in the eventual command. For live R06, at most four read-only lifecycle snapshots are allowed in addition to public event/latch observation; no polling loop or replay to chase the race. If the actual public observer cannot be installed under original scoped ownership, no block-start is delivered, cancellation/currentness loss cannot be independently observed, or disposal/drain cannot complete within the approved bounded run, preserve the corresponding HOLD. R06 uses cancellation as the representative live loss; the source-fiber, registration and service replacement losses are separate zero-inference lifecycle obligations L03-L05 and are not inferred from R06.
